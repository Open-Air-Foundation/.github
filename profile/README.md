# Open Air Foundation

The Open Air Foundation (OAF) builds open tools, trusted data, and practical resources that help communities understand and reduce airborne environmental health risks.

We believe communities, schools, researchers, public-interest organizations, and governments should have access to affordable and transparent tools for understanding the air people breathe.

Our work focuses on building infrastructure that others can **use, adapt, improve, and build upon**.

## What We Build

Our open-source work includes:

* **Open hardware** for affordable indoor and outdoor environmental monitoring
* **Firmware and embedded software** for air quality monitoring devices
* **Software and integrations** that make environmental data easier to access and use
* **Open data infrastructure** for collecting, sharing, and understanding measurements
* **Documentation and practical resources** that help others deploy and maintain these systems

These tools form part of a broader effort that also includes training, small grants, technical support, and partnerships with communities and public-interest organizations.

## Open by Design

Public infrastructure is more useful when people can understand how it works, adapt it to their needs, and build upon it.

We develop our hardware, software, data, and documentation openly and make them available under open licenses. Unless otherwise stated in an individual repository, Open Air Foundation materials are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) license.

This means you are free to use, share, and adapt our work, including for commercial purposes, as long as appropriate credit is given to the Open Air Foundation.
